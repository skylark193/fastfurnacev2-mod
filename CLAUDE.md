## Project Contract

Fast Furnace V2 is a NeoForge mod for Minecraft 26.1.2. Its registered content is:

- `fastfurnacev2:fast_cobblestone`: vanilla-style cobblestone with an animated lava-vein texture, a self-drop loot table, and use in the Fast Furnace crafting recipe.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_coal`: vanilla-style coal with an animated texture and ordinary fuel value of 1,600 ticks.
- `fastfurnacev2:extreme_coal_ore` and `deepslate_extreme_coal_ore`: regular/deepslate coal-ore variants with animated lava veins and independent deterministic 5% replacement rolls for matching vanilla ore in newly generated and once-processed existing chunks. They drop Extreme Coal with vanilla Fortune scaling, or themselves with Silk Touch, and each ore block crushes into three Extreme Coal.
- `fastfurnacev2:extreme_coal`: coal-shaped animated lava with an ordinary fuel value of 1,600 ticks. It doubles Fast Coal's added bonuses: +100 percentage points to active furnace/crusher speed, twice Fast Coal's added tier burn duration, and a 5× Accelerator boost supplying 7,200 work units.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_coal_block`: a reversible nine-Fast-Coal storage block with vanilla coal-block properties, an animated lava-vein texture, and ordinary fuel value of 16,000 ticks.
- `fastfurnacev2:extreme_coal_block`: a reversible nine-Extreme-Coal storage block with vanilla coal-block properties and an all-lava animated texture. Its ordinary fuel value is 16,000 ticks; it uses Extreme Coal's machine modifiers and supplies an 8,000-work, 5× Accelerator boost.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_iron_ore`, `fast_gold_ore`, `fast_diamond_ore`, and their `deepslate_` variants: regular/deepslate ore variants with animated lava veins and independent deterministic 5% replacement rolls for matching vanilla ore in newly generated and once-processed existing chunks. They drop Raw Fast Iron, Raw Fast Gold, or Raw Fast Diamond with vanilla Fortune scaling, or themselves with Silk Touch. Each ore block crushes into three matching crushed Fast materials.
- `fastfurnacev2:raw_fast_iron`, `raw_fast_gold`, and `raw_fast_diamond`: raw-material items with animated lava veins. Each smelts directly into one Fast Iron Ingot, Fast Gold Ingot, or Fast Diamond, and participates in dynamic crushing into two matching crushed items that each smelt into one finished material.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_iron_ingot`, `fast_gold_ingot`, and `fast_diamond`: finished Fast materials with animated lava veins, obtainable from raw/crushed smelting or Accelerator conversion. Nine craft a reversible storage block.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_iron_block`, `fast_gold_block`, and `fast_diamond_block`: reversible nine-material storage blocks with vanilla-block properties and animated lava veins. They replace vanilla iron, gold, and diamond blocks in furnace and crusher upgrade recipes and can be produced from ordinary storage blocks in the Accelerator.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_<material>_<tool>` for iron/gold/diamond and pickaxe/shovel/axe/sword: twelve vanilla-pattern tools using Fast materials and animated lava-vein textures. Iron tools break 20% faster; gold tools 30%; diamond tools 20% with a 5% chance to avoid durability damage. Swords attack 20%/30%/20% faster respectively, and the Fast Diamond Sword deals 20% more damage. Each set uses its finished Fast material for crafting and repair.
- `fastfurnacev2:accelerator`: a lava-surrounded Fast-material converter with an animated smooth-stone/lava-vein body and an eleven-stage top-face lightning bolt. It converts coal, iron ingots, gold ingots, and diamonds to Fast variants in 400 work ticks and their storage blocks to Fast storage blocks in 3,600. It runs without fuel; Fast Coal or its block provide a 3× boost with 3,600 or 4,000 work units, while Extreme Coal or its block provide a 5× boost with 7,200 or 8,000.
- `fastfurnacev2:extremely_fast_alloyer`: a three-input/one-output alloying machine with an all-lava animated body and the Accelerator's eleven-stage top bolt. Only Extreme Coal items fuel it, supplying 1,600 valid processing ticks each. Its independent shapeless recipe registry accepts no furnace, crusher, Accelerator, or crafting recipes. The built-in Fast Iron Ingot + Fast Gold Ingot + Fast Diamond operation produces one Extremely Fast Ingot in 800 base ticks, reduced by 100 per horizontal lava side to a 400-tick minimum. Craft the Alloyer by surrounding an Accelerator with eight Blocks of Extreme Coal.
- `fastfurnacev2:extremely_fast_ingot`: an ingot-silhouette item whose visible pixels are entirely animated lava, produced only by the built-in Extremely Fast Alloyer recipe and used to craft and repair Extremely Fast tools.
- `fastfurnacev2:extremely_fast_<tool>` for pickaxe/shovel/axe/sword: four vanilla-pattern, all-lava-textured tools using Extremely Fast Ingots. All have twice diamond durability. Mining tools break 50% faster, have built-in Fortune II, and independently roll 5% to double complete block drops. The sword attacks 50% faster with ordinary diamond base damage, has built-in Looting II, independently rolls 5% to double kill drops, and independently rolls 10% to deal double incoming damage before mitigation.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_furnace`, `iron_fast_furnace`, `gold_fast_furnace`, and `diamond_fast_furnace`: vanilla-compatible smelting furnaces with tiered speed, fuel-time, GUI, and optional JEI behavior.
- `fastfurnacev2:fast_crusher`, `iron_fast_crusher`, `gold_fast_crusher`, and `diamond_fast_crusher`: fuel-powered raw-ore crushers with the corresponding furnace tier's speed and no fuel-duration doubling. Each raw ore produces two matching crushed items instead of using its smelting result; each built-in regular/deepslate Fast or Extreme ore block produces three Extreme Coal or matching crushed Fast materials. Every completed operation independently rolls its tier's 10%/15%/20%/25% chance to double the complete result. A successful doubled result that cannot fit remains pending and pauses processing rather than falling back to normal output. They have animated Netherrack sides and bottoms plus top-down blenders that are static while idle and animated while lit; upgrade tiers add their metal block's texture around the blender housing. The base Fast Crusher uses three Fast Diamonds across the top row, a Fast Furnace in the center, and Fast Iron Ingots in the remaining five slots; upgrades surround the previous crusher with eight Blocks of Fast Iron, Fast Gold, then Fast Diamond.

| Tier | Normal / Fast / Extreme Coal speed | Standard recipe/operation, normal / Fast / Extreme | Furnace fuel doubling / crusher output doubling | Furnace Fast / Extreme Coal burn time before doubling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fast | 150% / 200% / 250% | 133 / 100 / 80 ticks | 10% | 2,400 / 3,200 ticks |
| Iron | 200% / 250% / 300% | 100 / 80 / 66 ticks | 15% | 2,800 / 4,000 ticks |
| Gold | 250% / 300% / 350% | 80 / 66 / 57 ticks | 20% | 3,200 / 4,800 ticks |
| Diamond | 300% / 350% / 400% | 66 / 57 / 50 ticks | 25% | 3,600 / 5,600 ticks |

In the four Fast Furnace tiers, each consumed fuel item independently rolls the tier's chance to double its adjusted burn time. Fast Crushers never make this roll; each completed crushing operation independently rolls its tier's 10%/15%/20%/25% chance to double its complete output: two raw-ore results become four, and three ore-block results become six. A successful roll remains pending until the complete doubled result fits, without consuming input or falling back to normal output. Fast Coal and Block of Fast Coal always add 50 percentage points to active processing speed; their duration multiplier increases by 25 percentage points per tier. Extreme Coal and its block add 100 percentage points and use `2 * scaleFastCoalBurnTime(base) - base`, doubling the added duration rather than the total. Extreme Coal's doubled burn durations are 6,400/8,000/9,600/11,200 ticks by tier. Before doubling, the Fast Coal block burns for 24,000/28,000/32,000/36,000 ticks and the Extreme Coal block for 32,000/40,000/48,000/56,000 ticks by tier.

All four Fast Furnace tiers and all four Fast Crusher tiers pause their active fuel countdown whenever no required result can be processed, including empty or unprocessable input, blocked output, and a pending crusher double that cannot fit. The countdown resumes with valid work without losing active Fast Coal or Extreme Coal state or consuming additional fuel merely to idle. When fuel expires during an operation, recipe progress remains saved without cooling down and resumes when replacement fuel is supplied.

Mining stone or blocks in `BlockTags.COAL_ORES` can add matching Fast Cobblestone or Fast Coal at a base 10% chance, plus 5 percentage points per Fortune level and capped at 100%. These are bonuses alongside normal drops, not replacements. Fast Furnace is crafted from eight Fast Cobblestone using the vanilla furnace pattern. Each furnace upgrade surrounds the previous tier with eight Blocks of Fast Iron, Fast Gold, then Fast Diamond in tier order. The Accelerator surrounds an Iron Fast Furnace with eight Redstone Blocks. The Extremely Fast Alloyer surrounds an Accelerator with eight Blocks of Extreme Coal. The base Fast Crusher recipe uses `DDD`/`IFI`/`III`, where `D` is Fast Diamond, `I` is Fast Iron Ingot, and `F` is Fast Furnace. Its three upgrades use the same Fast storage-block progression around the previous crusher tier. Both special-coal blocks and the three Fast material storage blocks use full 3×3 packing recipes and shapeless nine-item unpacking recipes. All twelve Fast tools use the corresponding vanilla stick-and-material pattern with Fast Iron Ingots, Fast Gold Ingots, or Fast Diamonds.

Other mods can use the public API under `com.skylark193.fastfurnacev2.api` to register ordinary and bonus fuels, crusher recipes, Accelerator conversions, and Accelerator boosts after default item components are bound. Registered definitions drive actual machine processing, automation, persistence, GUI synchronization, and JEI. Built-in fuels and recipes use the same implementation. Alloyer recipes currently use a separate internal built-in registry and are not part of this public API. See `docs/api.md` for the public contract and examples.

All active content appears in the Fast Furnace V2 creative tab, whose icon is Fast Cobblestone. Fast Cobblestone and all five Fast/Extreme storage blocks also appear in Building Blocks; Fast Coal, Extreme Coal, raw and finished Fast materials, the Extremely Fast Ingot, and active crushed ores appear in Ingredients; all eight regular/deepslate Extreme and Fast material ores appear in Natural Blocks; all furnaces, crushers, the Accelerator, and the Extremely Fast Alloyer appear in Functional Blocks; all sixteen pickaxes, shovels, and axes appear in Tools & Utilities; and all sixteen swords and axes appear in Combat. A provisional generated crushed item replaced by a later external match can remain registered, but is intentionally excluded from creative contents and dynamic resources.

The mod also discovers vanilla and modded raw ore items during registration and selects one crushed result per source. Discovery recognizes conventional `raw_...`/`..._raw`/`..._raw_ore` IDs and direct or nested entries in packaged `c:raw_materials` item tags. Matching external crushed items or dusts are reused instead of duplicated; conventional IDs and packaged `c:dusts`/`c:crushed_ores` tags supply candidates. Generated variants use a shared dust-pile model tinted from the source item texture. Generated ordinary smelting recipes preserve the raw recipe's output, experience, cooking time, category, and group, while existing external smelting recipes are reused, so both kinds work in vanilla and Fast Furnaces.

### JEI validation boundary

JEI APIs are `compileOnly`. JEI 29.2.0.21 declares Minecraft `[26.1,26.1]` and cannot launch on Minecraft 26.1.2, so normal development launches exclude its runtime. Compilation and dependency resolution are not JEI runtime validation.

After selecting a JEI release compatible with Minecraft and the integration API, update `jei_version` and, if needed, `jei_minecraft_version`, then opt in with `-PwithJei`. The Gradle check uses property presence: even `-PwithJei=false` enables `localRuntime`. Never bypass JEI's own version requirement or claim runtime validation without launching it.

## Architecture Map

## Implementation Invariants

### Registration, drops, and resources

- Register every `DeferredRegister` with the mod event bus. Add translations for new registry objects and user-facing text.
- Fast Cobblestone explicitly mirrors vanilla cobblestone: stone map color, base-drum instrument, `requiresCorrectToolForDrops()`, and strength 2.0/6.0. The mineable tag supplies pickaxe behavior; its loot table explicitly drops Fast Cobblestone.
- Do not use `BlockBehaviour.Properties.ofFullCopy(Blocks.COBBLESTONE)`: it also copies vanilla's loot-table key and description ID, causing incorrect drops or translations.
- `FastBonusDrops` runs during NeoForge's server-side `BlockDropsEvent`, after vanilla drops are calculated. Require a non-creative player breaker, enabled block drops, a correct tool, and no Silk Touch. `getDropChance` starts at 10% and adds 5 percentage points per Fortune level, capped at 100% for nonstandard levels. Append to `event.getDrops()`; never clear or replace normal drops. `BlockTags.COAL_ORES` covers coal ore and deepslate coal ore.
- Fast Coal uses default vanilla item properties and `data/neoforge/data_maps/item/furnace_fuels.json` with `burn_time: 1600`. Only Fast Furnace applies its longer duration and speed boost.
- Regular and Deepslate Extreme Coal Ore explicitly mirror the corresponding vanilla coal ore's physical properties without copying its loot-table key or description ID. Regular strength is 3.0/3.0 and deepslate strength is 4.5/3.0. Their loot tables follow vanilla coal ore's Silk Touch alternatives and `minecraft:ore_drops` Fortune formula, dropping Extreme Coal instead of vanilla coal. Extreme Coal has ordinary `burn_time: 1600`; only the custom machines apply its larger bonuses.
- Regular and deepslate Fast Iron, Gold, and Diamond Ores use explicit properties rather than `ofFullCopy`: regular strength 3.0/3.0, deepslate strength 4.5/3.0, and the corresponding stone/deepslate map color. Their loot tables drop the corresponding raw Fast item using `minecraft:ore_drops` and Silk Touch alternatives. Regular/deepslate Fast Iron Ore and Block of Fast Iron belong in `needs_stone_tool`; both forms of Fast Gold Ore and Fast Diamond Ore and their storage blocks belong in `needs_iron_tool`. Every one also requires the pickaxe-minable tag.
- Fast material storage blocks explicitly mirror their vanilla counterparts' physical properties: Fast Iron uses metal map color, iron xylophone, and strength 5.0/6.0; Fast Gold uses gold map color, bell, and strength 3.0/6.0; Fast Diamond uses diamond map color and strength 5.0/6.0. Their loot tables drop themselves. Packing and unpacking recipes are reversible, and their block items do not use fuel data maps.
- `FastOreGeneration` handles both new and existing chunks through server-side `ChunkEvent.Load`. Each regular or deepslate `minecraft:coal_ore`, `iron_ore`, `gold_ore`, or `diamond_ore` position gets an independent deterministic 5% roll derived from the world seed, block position, and its source block's fixed salt; regular and deepslate sources have separate salts. Replacement results remain `DeferredBlock<Block>` instances until processing so class initialization does not resolve registry entries early. Skip sections whose palettes cannot contain an eligible ore and mutate only the supplied chunk with update flags 0. Store serializable Boolean `fast_ore_chunk_processed` and `deepslate_fast_ore_chunk_processed` attachments after their independent passes and call `markUnsaved()` after setting them. The separate markers let chunks processed by 1.3.0 receive only the new deepslate pass without rerolling regular ores. Retain the legacy `extreme_coal_chunk_processed` attachment registration for old saves, but do not let it suppress either current pass. Position-derived randomness keeps results independent of chunk load order.
- Block of Fast Coal explicitly mirrors vanilla coal blocks: black map color, base-drum instrument, `requiresCorrectToolForDrops()`, and strength 5.0/6.0. The mineable tag supplies pickaxe behavior; its loot table explicitly drops itself. Do not use `ofFullCopy(Blocks.COAL_BLOCK)`, which would inherit vanilla's loot-table key and description ID.

Do not replace raw or external recipes or hardcode result items.

### Accelerator processing and synchronization

- The Accelerator is deliberately separate from `FastFurnaceTier`, ordinary smelting recipes, and the vanilla recipe book. Built-in item conversions are coal → Fast Coal, iron ingot → Fast Iron Ingot, gold ingot → Fast Gold Ingot, and diamond → Fast Diamond in 400 work units; coal, iron, gold, and diamond blocks convert to their Fast storage blocks in 3,600. All are registered through the public API; third-party conversions use the same runtime path. Do not register ordinary fuel data or smelting JSON for these operations.
- Require lava in every `Direction.Plane.HORIZONTAL` neighbor through `FluidTags.LAVA`; source and flowing lava both count, while above and below are irrelevant. Processing, fuel consumption, and boost drain occur only with all four sides and room for the required result. Missing lava or a blocked output preserves progress and boost. Empty/unsupported input resets progress, and changing input type resets it without discarding banked boost.
- Normal work advances one unit per tick. Fast Coal supplies 3,600 units and its block 4,000; their active boost advances up to three per tick. Extreme Coal supplies 7,200 units and its block 8,000; both advance up to five per tick, doubling Fast Coal's +2× added speed to +4× rather than producing 6× total speed. Clamp each step by remaining recipe work and boost work. This gives exactly nine/ten boosted coal conversions from Fast Coal/its block, exactly 18 coal or two coal-block conversions from Extreme Coal, and exactly 20 coal or two coal-block plus two item conversions from Block of Extreme Coal. Boosted item operations take 134/80 ticks and boosted block operations 1,200/720 ticks without wasting partial ticks.
- The Accelerator works without fuel. When valid work starts with no banked boost, consume one API-registered boost item and store its work capacity, work-per-tick, and definition ID. Ordinary coal and other unregistered furnace fuels must be rejected by menu slots, shift-click routing, `canPlaceItem`, and automation. Hoppers insert input from above, extract results below, and insert accepted boost fuel from horizontal sides.
- Persist `accelerator_progress`, `accelerator_recipe_ticks`, `accelerator_boost_work`, `accelerator_total_boost_work`, `accelerator_boost_fuel`, and legacy `accelerator_extreme_boost`. Eight menu values expose progress, recipe duration, remaining/total boost, horizontal lava count, current processing state, extreme-boost identity, and effective boost work per tick; all current values fit ordinary synchronized integers. Old saves without a boost ID use the extreme flag to select Extreme Coal, or Fast Coal otherwise so legacy Fast Coal and Fast Coal Block boosts retain 3× speed.
- NeoForge item transactions restore stack snapshots but not custom conversion progress. In `onTransfer`, detect a committed empty/fill input transition with `currentAmount == 0 || currentAmount == amountChange`, journal the deferred reset through `SnapshotJournal`, and apply it only in `onRootCommit`. Do not reset progress from `setItem` while `insideTransaction` is true; otherwise an aborted extraction restores coal but erases its progress. Direct non-transactional input changes still reset immediately.
- `AcceleratorBlock.BOLT_LEVEL` ranges from 0–10 and uses ceiling progress so the last partial decile displays the full bolt before output resets to zero. The inherited `LIT` state means work advanced on that tick, not merely that boost remains banked. Blockstates select facing and bolt level while ignoring `lit`; all eleven top textures and the shared smooth-stone body have animated lava metadata. Models use `minecraft:block/orientable_with_bottom` with the bolt texture on `top` and the body on `front`, `side`, and `bottom`; ordinary `orientable` would incorrectly copy the bolt onto the bottom.
- The Accelerator is crafted by surrounding an Iron Fast Furnace with eight Redstone Blocks. Its built-in and API-registered conversions and exact-work boosts use dedicated JEI categories rather than ordinary smelting or Fast Furnace categories; do not route them into ordinary smelting transfers or categories, because Accelerator conversions and boost rules are custom.

### Extremely Fast Alloyer processing and synchronization

Its recipe storage is internal and currently exposes no third-party registration event.

The built-in `fastfurnacev2:alloyer/extremely_fast_ingot` recipe consumes one Fast Iron Ingot, Fast Gold Ingot, and Fast Diamond, producing one Extremely Fast Ingot with an 800-tick base duration. Each horizontal lava side subtracts 100 ticks to a 400-tick minimum, recalculated dynamically without resetting elapsed work. No special-coal bonus, tier scaling, or Accelerator work/boost applies.

Any ordering works, but missing ingredients, duplicate substitutions, partial recipes, and unsupported items must not match. Menu slots, shift-click routing, `canPlaceItem`, and automation accept only registered Alloyer ingredients; matching a furnace/crusher/Accelerator ingredient elsewhere does not make it an Alloyer input.

Consume one coal only when valid work can advance with no banked fuel, supplying exactly 1,600 valid processing ticks. Advance one work unit and decrement the burn countdown only while a valid recipe advances and its required result fits. Consume one item from each matched slot and insert the complete result only when processing finishes. Missing fuel or a blocked output preserves progress and inputs without advancing; empty/invalid input resets work but preserves banked burn time. Direct input changes and committed empty/fill transaction transitions reset work. With unchanged stacked inputs, available fuel, and output space, processing continues automatically.

Do not reset from `setItem` while `insideTransaction` is true; an aborted extraction must restore both items and progress.
- Slots 0–2 are inputs, slot 3 is Extreme Coal fuel, and slot 4 is the result. The top exposes only the three inputs, horizontal faces expose only the fuel slot, and the bottom exposes only the result; output insertion is rejected. Six menu values expose work progress, current lava-adjusted recipe ticks, whether work advanced on the current tick, remaining/total burn time, and horizontal lava count. Models use `minecraft:block/orientable_with_bottom`; the stage texture belongs only on top, and the all-lava body belongs on front, sides, and bottom. Blockstates ignore `lit`, as the Accelerator's do.
- The Alloyer uses the same explicit stone/base-drum properties and 3.5 strength as the Accelerator, requires the pickaxe-minable tag, drops itself, and copies only `minecraft:custom_name`. Its survival recipe is eight Blocks of Extreme Coal around an Accelerator.

### Furnace ticking and synchronization

- One `BlockEntityType` accepts all four furnace blocks and all four crusher blocks and is constructed with this version's public `BlockEntityType` constructor; `BlockEntityType.Builder` is unavailable. The block entity derives its immutable tier and crusher identity from the actual block. Each crusher uses its corresponding `FastFurnaceTier`, shares that tier's furnace menu and screen, and gets its default display name from its actual block rather than its tier.
- Each block uses a codec capturing both its tier and furnace/crusher identity; a shared codec that always constructs the base tier or a furnace would decode upgrades incorrectly.
- The ticker must use `FAST_FURNACE_BLOCK_ENTITY` and call `FastFurnaceBlockEntity.serverTick` through `createTickerHelper`. Vanilla `createFurnaceTicker` would bypass custom timing.
- Retain the custom `openContainer` block-entity check; vanilla `FurnaceBlock` specifically checks `FurnaceBlockEntity`.
- `FastFurnaceBlockEntity` extends `AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity` with `RecipeType.SMELTING`. Preserve vanilla recipes, other fuels, inventory, XP, comparators, fuel remainders, facing, light level 13 while lit, and custom-name drops.
- Scale cooking through `FastFurnaceTier.scaleCookTime`: 150/200/250/300% normally, an additional 50 percentage points with active Fast Coal, or an additional 100 with Extreme Coal. Standard normal-fuel recipes take 133/100/80/66 ticks; Fast Coal recipes take 100/80/66/57 ticks; Extreme Coal recipes take 80/66/57/50 ticks.
- Apply Fast Coal's tier duration multiplier through `scaleFastCoalBurnTime`, or Extreme Coal's doubled added duration through `scaleExtremeCoalBurnTime`, then apply the independent tier-specific doubling roll only for actual Fast Furnace tiers. Every Fast Crusher tier must skip the fuel roll entirely. Before doubling, Fast Coal burns for 2,400/2,800/3,200/3,600 ticks and Extreme Coal for 3,200/4,000/4,800/5,600. Do not globally alter either item's ordinary fuel value.
- Fast Crushers derive their immutable identity from `FastFurnaceBlock.isFastCrusher()`, captured by the actual block's codec, not from the shared tier. Resolve input through `FastFurnaceApi.findCrusherRecipe`; built-in raw-item definitions use `CrushedOres.forRawOre` and produce `FAST_CRUSHER_OUTPUT_COUNT` (two) matching crushed items. Eight additional built-ins under `crusher/ore/` accept the regular/deepslate Extreme and Fast material ore blocks and produce three Extreme Coal or matching crushed Fast materials, while API definitions supply their own input/output counts. Never use the raw smelting result or accept an ordinary smelting input without a registered crusher recipe. A zero custom processing time inherits the raw smelting recipe's `cookingTime`, falling back to `BURN_TIME_STANDARD` when absent, and tier/fuel speed scaling remains unchanged.
- On each completed crushing operation only, roll `tier.getDoubleBurnChance()` (10%/15%/20%/25%) once to double the complete result: four items for built-in raw-item recipes, six for built-in ore-block recipes, or twice an API recipe's output count. This deliberately reuses the tier's furnace fuel-doubling percentage as the crusher output-doubling chance. If the doubled result fits, consume the required input and output everything together. Otherwise persist `fast_crusher_double_output_pending`, hold progress at the final tick, require the doubled result in `canProcess`, and pause fuel until it fits. Resume that same doubled result without rerolling or consuming input early; clear pending state on successful output or a changed input. Ordinary furnaces and Fast Furnace tiers must not consume output-roll randomness or double results.
- Item-stack snapshots do not include `fast_crusher_double_output_pending`. For crusher input transactions, journal a deferred pending-double reset and apply it on root commit after an empty/fill transition, while retaining vanilla's journaled cooking reset through `super.onTransfer`. An aborted extraction must preserve both the input and pending four-item result; a committed extraction/refill must permit a fresh output roll.
- Decrement `currentBurnTime` only when the required nonempty result passes `canBurn`; a pending crusher double checks the complete doubled result rather than its base output. Empty or unprocessable input and blocked output pause the countdown without clearing the active special-fuel state or consuming more fuel. Keep the decrement before final-tick expiration and replacement-fuel consumption in the valid-work branch so existing processing timing remains unchanged. Banked fuel remains `LIT` while paused.
- Do not reset or cool down `DATA_COOKING_PROGRESS` when fuel expires or no replacement fuel is available while processable input remains. Preserve the saved progress through the vanilla block-entity data and resume it on refueling; the existing special-coal speed transition may rescale progress to preserve its fraction. Clearing or changing the input still resets progress.
- Use the matched `FastFuel` from the stack actually consumed for duration scaling and active speed, not the current fuel slot, which may already hold different fuel. Persist its ID as `active_fuel`; retain legacy `fast_coal_burning` and `extreme_coal_burning` for old-save fallback and built-in menu compatibility. Both Fast Coal IDs map to menu type 1 and both Extreme Coal IDs, including `EXTREME_COAL_BLOCK_FUEL_ID`, map to type 2 in synchronization and legacy save flags; treating the Extreme block as a custom or Fast fuel makes the GUI report the wrong bonus family. Clear active fuel when burn time expires. `FastFurnaceV2.isFastCoalFuel` and `isExtremeCoalFuel` remain built-in item helpers, not general custom-fuel detection.
- Exact remaining and total burn times are stored under `lit_time_remaining` and `lit_total_time` and mirrored into vanilla data for furnace behavior.
- `FastFurnaceMenu` has eleven data slots. Indices 0–3 retain vanilla behavior; 4–7 contain low/high halves of the exact remaining and total fuel times; index 8 is 0 ordinary/1 built-in Fast Coal/2 Extreme Coal/3 custom; index 9 identifies Fast Crusher; index 10 is effective processing speed including the active custom or built-in bonus. `isFastCoalActive()` remains the general special-fuel check for existing callers, so check `isExtremeCoalActive()` first when built-in bonus amounts differ, and use `getSpeedPercent()` for arbitrary speeds. The crusher flag comes from the server block entity's immutable block type and is read-only in its custom server data. Use split integers instead of vanilla short-value normalization or a custom per-tick packet.

Regular coal displays approximately 12.03/16/20/24.24 smelts; Fast Coal displays 24/35/48.48/63.16, its block 240/350/484.85/631.58, Extreme Coal 40/60.61/84.21/112, and its block 400/606.06/842.11/1120 before any random doubling roll.
- Do not modify `RecipeTypes.SMELTING_FUEL` to implement Fast Furnace durations; that would incorrectly change ordinary furnace displays.

### GameTests and textures

Vanilla's `TestFunctionLoader` bootstraps before mod construction, so registering a loader during mod/class initialization is too late. `DirectGameTestInstance` invokes the mod's mapped functions directly.

- Use the square sources under `tools/texture_sources/`; never feed a generated animation strip back into the generator. It writes both the vertical PNG strip and sibling `.png.mcmeta`.
